# force_field_parameters

Check literature LJ parameters (sigma and epsilon) of the force fields UFF [1] and DREIDING [2] and compare a given force field to them.

[1] Rappe, A. K., Casewit, C. J., Colwell, K. S., Goddard, W. A. I. & Skiff, W. M. UFF, a full periodic table force field for molecular mechanics and molecular dynamics simulations. J. Am. Chem. Soc. 114, 10024–10035 (1992).

[2] The van der Waals Parameters for DREIDING" in "1.  Mayo, S. L., Olafson, B. D. & Goddard, W. A. DREIDING:  a generic force field for molecular simulations. J. Phys. Chem. 94, 8897–8909 (1990)
